You control HOME. When AWAY has the ball, choose Man Coverage, Zone Coverage, or Blitz. In Zone, the blue defensive line stays near the line of scrimmage while linebackers and defensive backs guard separate areas of the field. On a Blitz, blue D-line and linebackers rush the quarterback, while offensive linemen slow and redirect linebackers before they can get around the block. Touching a quarterback before the throw or handoff is a sack. After the ball leaves the QB, rushers chase the ball carrier or pass target instead. Interception rules stay the same. A blue player can stop a red return (and vice versa) with one touch.
